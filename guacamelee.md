Recipe for Guacamole:

Ingredients:
- 2 avocados
- 2 lime
- 2 tsp salt
- 3 jalapeños
- 1 tomato
- bunch of cilantro

Instructions:
- cut and mash avocados
- chop onion to bits
- mince leaves and stems and turn cilantro to green mush
- slice and dice tomato to raw chunks
- mince the jalapeños until someone cries
- squeeze the guts out of the limes
- throw salt
- and punch well
